ENTRY REQUIREMENTS (Summary)
ALL UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMMES
● WASSCE and SSSCE holders with credit passes (A1-C6)/(A-D) respectively in three (3) core subjects: English Language, Mathematics, and Integrated Science/Social Studies, and three (3) elective subjects relevant to the programme of study.

● ADVANCED BUSINESS CERTIFICATE EXAMINATION (ABCE) holders with passes in three (3) compulsory subjects plus three (3) optional subjects relevant to the Programme of study.

● GENERAL BUSINESS CERTIFICATE (GBCE) holders with credit passes (A-D) in six (6) subjects comprising three core subjects including English Language and Mathematics plus three (3) relevant elective subjects.

● ADVANCED LEVEL holders with credit in at least Five (5) subjects at the GCE ‘O’ Level (or its equivalent) including English and Mathematics, plus at least two passes in the subjects relevant to the programme of study.

● HIGHER NATIONAL DIPLOMA (HND) Holders with relevant working experience in addition to the minimum entry requirements at WASSCE/SSSCE or Five (5) GCE O’ Level Credits including English Language and Mathematics. Applicants may be asked to write an entrance examination and/or attend an interview as part of the admission process.

● DIPLOMA HOLDERS with University of Energy and Natural Resources Diplomas or recognised equivalents with relevant working experience may be considered for admission. Applicants may be asked to write an entrance examination and/or attend an interview as part of the admission process. Applicants will be admitted to either Level 200 or Level 300 based on their FGPA and area of study.

● OTHER QUALIFICATIONS: Other qualifications acceptable for consideration include International Baccalaureate (IB), IGCSE, American Grade 13 Examinations and other external qualifications which have equivalences to the WASSCE/SSSCE and the GCE (A Levels).

● FOREIGN APPLICANTS who have any of the above qualifications or their equivalences from an accredited/recognised institution.
NB: International students must submit their certificates to the Ghana Tertiary Education Commission (GTEC) for evaluation and attach the report(s) to the application form.

Mature Applicants
● Applicants must be at least 25 years old and show proof of age with a birth certificate or any legitimate documentary proof of date of birth.

● Must pass Mature Students’ Entrance Examinations conducted by the institution itself (English Language, Mathematics, and an Aptitude Test).

● Possess any of the following:

SSSCE/WASSCE/GCE “O” or “A” Level, ABCE, GBCE and other equivalences
Teacher’s Cert. ‘A’
NVTI/NABPTEX Certificates

ALL DIPLOMA PROGRAMMES
WASSCE holders with credit passes (A1-C6) in English Language, Mathematics, and Integrated Science/Social Studies plus two credit passes (A1-C6) in two elective subjects relevant to the programme of study.
