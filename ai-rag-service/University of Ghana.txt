Ghanaian Applicants with WASSCE/SSSCE Qualification

A. General Entry Requirements into the Full-Time Degree Programmes
An applicant for admission to an undergraduate degree programme in the University of Ghana must have at least credits (A1 - C6 in WASSCE or A - D in SSSCE) in the following subjects:
• The Four Core Subjects: English, Core Mathematics, Integrated Science/Core Science, and Social Studies.
• Three elective subjects.


B. Calculation of Aggregates for Admission
The total aggregate of results, comprising six subjects (relevant core and electives), should not exceed:
• Aggregate 24 for Regular admissions.
• Aggregate 30 for Distance Education admissions.
For Science-Related Disciplines:
• Aggregate is calculated using English, Core Mathematics, Integrated Science/Core Science, and three elective subjects in the Sciences (refer to specific programme elective requirements below).
• Note: Social Studies credit is excluded in the calculation.
For Non-Science Disciplines:
• Aggregate is calculated using English, Core Mathematics, Social Studies/Life Skills, and three elective subjects in General Arts/Business (refer to specific programme elective requirements below).
• Note: Integrated Science/Core Science credit is excluded in the calculation.

NOTICES: 

Application Deadline
The application deadline for the following undergraduate programmes (Level 100 entry) under the College of Health Sciences for the 2026/2027 academic year is yet to be determined: Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBChB), Bachelor of Dental Surgery, Doctor of Pharmacy, BSc. Nursing, BSc. Midwifery, BSc. Medical Laboratory Sciences, BSc. Physiotherapy, BSc. Dietetics, BSc. Diagnostic Radiography, BSc. Occupational Therapy and BSc. Respiratory Therapy. Applicants for these programmes will write a computer-based entrance examination before the release of WASSCE results. WASSCE results are not required to apply for or sit the entrance examination.  Applications submitted after 24 July 2026 will not be accepted. Details of the entrance examinations will be available on the Admissions Portal to applicants for these programmes and via SMS. Admission decisions will be based on the entrance examination performance and WASSCE results. Applicants who fail to pass the entrance examinations may revise their choices to select other programmes before general application deadlines close.

Entrance Examination
The entrance examination for these programmes will be held from August 3 to August 8, 2026. Applicants are to submit their applications with accurate and up-to-date contact details by 24 July 2026, to facilitate effective communication of specific entry exam details via SMS and on the application portal.
